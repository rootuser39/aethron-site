How I Work

I treat infrastructure as a system: compute, network, security, and cost are interdependent, not siloed. When a GPU throttles, I don't just check the hardware—I trace the network path, inspect queue depths, correlate with workload patterns. I build observability into everything I design because you can't optimize what you can't measure. I use automation not to replace judgment, but to free up time for root cause analysis and strategic thinking. Reliability isn't just about uptime—it's about graceful degradation, fast recovery, and economics. I focus on outcomes: reduced MTTR, cost efficiency, predictable performance. No fluff, no buzzwords, just systems that work.
